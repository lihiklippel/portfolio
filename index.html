UIUX Manager for the cybersecurity company dbdome

In the DBDOME project, I led the design of all user interfaces for an advanced cybersecurity platform, implementing modern, user-centered UI/UX principles. The process involved crafting the user experience to enable attack detection, risk management, and proactive response in an intuitive and secure manner.

I applied the core values of user experience design:

  • Clarity and Accessibility: Designed screens that are easy to read and interpret, even under critical information load.
  • Intuitiveness: Logical flow of actions allowing users to quickly identify threats and take immediate action.
  • Information Prioritization: Displayed critical data prominently, with visual hierarchy supporting fast decision-making.
  • Real-Time Feedback: Designed interfaces that provide clear, immediate event notifications, enabling users to respond swiftly and safely.
  • Professional Aesthetics: Combined modern design that reinforces user trust in a mission-critical cybersecurity system.

Through this project, I transformed the entire user experience into a natural, goal-focused, and secure workflow, balancing complex information with high usability.
